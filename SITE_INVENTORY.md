## 3. Interactivity & Animations
- **Scroll Reveals:** Sections, text, and cards fade/slide in when scrolled into view.
- **Hero Parallax:** Mouse move creates subtle parallax on the hero elements.
- **Navbar:** Sticky, changes background when scrolled (`is-scrolled` class).
- **Background Effects:** `premium-dot-field` dynamic background dots scaling and drifting.
- **Forms:** Client-side validation, disabled while submitting, success states.
